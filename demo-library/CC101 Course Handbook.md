## Assessment

| Component | Weight | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly quizzes (best 8 of 10) | 10% | Weeks 2-11, online |
| Lab exercises (5) | 20% | Weeks 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 |
| Group project | 30% | Proposal week 6, demo week 13 |
| Final exam | 40% | Week 15, closed book |

To pass the course you need at least 50% overall **and** at least 40% on the final exam.

### Quizzes

Each quiz opens on Monday at 09:00 and closes 7 days later, on the next Monday at 08:59. A quiz has 10 questions and a 20-minute time limit once started. Only your first attempt counts. Quiz 1 opens on Monday 17 August 2026.

### Lab exercises

Labs are submitted as a zip file of your code plus a one-page screenshot report. Each lab is due at 23:59 on the Sunday after its lab session.

### Late submissions

Labs and the group project lose **10% of the marks available per day late**, for up to 3 days. Work more than 3 days late gets zero. Quizzes cannot be taken late. An extension needs a medical certificate or a letter from the student services office, sent to the course coordinator before the deadline.

## Group project

Teams of four, all from the same section. Each team deploys a small web application to the cloud and presents it.

1. **Proposal** (week 6, 5% of the course): one page naming the application, the cloud services you will use and an estimated monthly cost.
2. **Demo** (week 13, 20% of the course): a 10-minute live demo and 5 minutes of questions.
3. **Peer review** (week 13, 5% of the course): each member rates the others' contribution. A member rated below 2 out of 5 by every teammate may receive a lower individual mark.

The application must stay within the free tier of the cloud provider you choose. Teams pay for any charges themselves; the course does not refund cloud bills.

## Academic integrity

You may discuss ideas with classmates, but code and reports you submit must be your own team's work. Using an AI assistant is allowed if you say which one and for what in your report.

If you suspect an integrity breach:

1. Write down what you saw, with the date and the assessment it concerns.
2. Send it to the course coordinator, not to the teaching assistants.
3. The coordinator replies within 5 working days and decides whether to refer it to the integrity committee.

A confirmed first breach on a lab gives zero for that lab. A confirmed breach on the final exam is always referred to the integrity committee.
